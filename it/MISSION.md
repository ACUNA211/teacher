# Mission: IT and certs

## Why
Get stronger in my IT specialist role and earn CompTIA **A+ → Network+ → Security+**. The long-term payoff: helping secure company data as we grow.

## Success looks like
- Pass A+ Core 1 (220-1201) around **February 2027**, then Core 2 (220-1202).
- Then Network+, then Security+.
- Explain *why* an account or access problem happens (network, device, identity) instead of only following a fix-it script.
- Connect exam topics to real Entra ID / Intune work: device enrollment, compliance, sign-in problems.

## Constraints
- Learning alongside a full-time job: daily lessons stay **short** (about 10 minutes).
- Saturday quiz goes deeper and mixes older topics in.
- The site is public: work examples are always generalized (see root `CLAUDE.md`).

## Out of scope
- Microsoft certifications (undecided; revisit after Security+).
- Deep hardware repair beyond what A+ asks for.
