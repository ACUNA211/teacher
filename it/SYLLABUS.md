# A+ Core 1 (220-1201) pacing

Target: sit Core 1 in **late February 2027**. About 21 weeks from 2026-09-26.
Source of truth for scope: the official CompTIA exam objectives (see `RESOURCES.md`). Topic order uses Professor Messer's free 220-1201 course (63 videos).

Exam domains (weight): Mobile Devices 13% · Networking 23% · Hardware 25% · Virtualization & Cloud 11% · Hardware & Network Troubleshooting 28%.

Order puts networking first: it's the most useful domain for daily work and the bridge to Network+.

| Weeks | Dates (approx) | Focus | Objectives |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–4 | Sep 27 – Oct 24 | Networking | 2.1–2.8 |
| 5–6 | Oct 25 – Nov 7 | Mobile devices (incl. MDM → Intune tie-ins) | 1.1–1.3 |
| 7 | Nov 8 – Nov 14 | Virtualization & cloud | 4.1–4.2 |
| 8–13 | Nov 15 – Dec 26 | Hardware (lighter week over holidays) | 3.1–3.8 |
| 14–17 | Dec 27 – Jan 23 | Troubleshooting (interleaved with earlier domains) | 5.1–5.6 |
| 18–21 | Jan 24 – Feb 20 | Review, practice exams, weak spots | all |

Daily lesson = one small slice of one objective, plus 2–3 retrieval questions from earlier lessons.
Saturday quiz = this week + older weeks, interleaved.
Behind schedule? Say so in the Saturday quiz and propose a new date. Don't silently compress lessons.
