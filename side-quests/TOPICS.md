When a topic reaches **about 3 lessons**, **or** the user states a concrete goal for it, **suggest** (don't force) giving it its own workspace with its own `MISSION.md`. If they agree: create `<topic>/`, move its lessons, interview for the mission, add it to the root `CLAUDE.md` routing table and `data/hub.js`.
