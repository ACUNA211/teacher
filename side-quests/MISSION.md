# Mission: Side quests

## Why
Explore whatever catches my interest (politics, game design, and so on) without cluttering the main tracks.

## Success looks like
- Satisfying, self-contained lessons on topics I'm curious about.
- Topics that turn into real goals graduate to their own workspace (see `TOPICS.md`).

## Constraints
- No schedule: lessons only when I ask.

## Out of scope
- Anything that belongs to an existing track.
